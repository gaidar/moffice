Every skill has exactly one owner. A function can have skills owned by a second role when the second role is the marketing reviewer. Every agent role drafts and recommends, and you, the marketer, decide.

## The 14 roles

| Agent role | Mission | Owned functions | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Head of marketing](../agents/head-of-marketing.md) | Runs the cadence, sets priorities, reviews work against goals, owns planning and budget | Budget and planning | 3 |
| [Product marketing manager](../agents/product-marketing-manager.md) | Turns the product into a position the market understands and sales can use | Positioning and messaging; competitive intelligence; go-to-market and launches; product marketing and sales enablement | 9 |
| [Insights analyst](../agents/insights-analyst.md) | Defines who to sell to, based on evidence the marketer supplies | Segmentation, ICP and personas | 4 |
| [Demand generation manager](../agents/demand-generation-manager.md) | Designs campaigns and nurture that turn attention into pipeline | Campaign design | 2 |
| [Content lead](../agents/content-lead.md) | Plans and writes web, blog, social, email and video content | Content | 6 |
| [SEO specialist](../agents/seo-specialist.md) | Earns search traffic from the buyers in the ICP | SEO | 3 |
| [Paid media manager](../agents/paid-media-manager.md) | Plans, writes and adjusts paid campaigns within budget | Paid demand gen | 3 |
| [Events and webinar manager](../agents/events-and-webinar-manager.md) | Plans events and webinars and makes sure leads get followed up | Events and webinars | 3 |
| [PR and communications manager](../agents/pr-and-communications-manager.md) | Handles announcements, media outreach and difficult moments | PR and comms | 3 |
| [Partner marketing manager](../agents/partner-marketing-manager.md) | Builds joint marketing with partners and equips them to sell | Partner and channel marketing | 2 |
| [Brand and creative director](../agents/brand-and-creative-director.md) | Keeps the brand voice and writes briefs for designers | Brand and design briefs | 2 |
| [Marketing analyst](../agents/marketing-analyst.md) | Reads performance, evaluates campaigns and says what to do next | Analytics and campaign evaluation | 4 |
| [Marketing operations manager](../agents/marketing-operations-manager.md) | Keeps process, tools, tracking and lead handoff working | Marketing ops | 3 |

## Coverage check

Each of the 16 functions has one owning agent role.

| Function | Owning agent role |
|---|---|
| Positioning and messaging | Product marketing manager |
| Segmentation, ICP and personas | Insights analyst |
| Competitive intelligence | Product marketing manager |
| Go-to-market and launches | Product marketing manager |
| Campaign design | Demand generation manager |
| Content (web, blog, social, email, video scripts) | Content lead |
| SEO | SEO specialist |
| Paid demand gen | Paid media manager |
| Events and webinars | Events and webinar manager |
| PR and comms | PR and communications manager |
| Product marketing and sales enablement | Product marketing manager |
| Partner and channel marketing | Partner marketing manager |
| Brand and design briefs | Brand and creative director |
| Analytics and campaign evaluation | Marketing analyst |
| Marketing ops | Marketing operations manager |
| Budget and planning | Head of marketing |

The marketing reviewer owns no function outright. It owns review skills that sit inside other functions.

## Review versus evaluation

Two roles judge work, and they judge different things.

- The marketing reviewer judges a draft before it is used: is it accurate against the context pack, and does it meet the skill's checklist?
- The marketing analyst judges results after they happen: what did the campaign produce, and what should change?
