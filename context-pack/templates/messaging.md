## Positioning

**Positioning statement**

Guidance: For (audience) who (need), (product) is a (category) that (main benefit), because (reason). Unlike (alternative), it (difference).

Example: For learning leaders at knowledge-work companies, CogniATion Coach trains people to think problems through.
