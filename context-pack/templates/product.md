**Purpose:** What you sell, how it is priced, and the limits a skill must respect when it describes the product.

Do not fill with guesses – leave blank and the skills will ask.

The examples below describe a fictional company. Delete each guidance and example line as you fill in your answer, or leave them. Skills read only the answers.

## What it is

**Product name**

Guidance: The exact name, with its capitalization.

Example: CogniATion Coach (the Coach on second mention)

Answer:

**Category**

Guidance: What a buyer would call this kind of product.

Example: Critical thinking training software

Answer:

**What it does**

Guidance: Two or three plain sentences describing what a customer can do with it.

Example: The Coach is a chatbot that questions employees about a work problem instead of answering it, then scores their reasoning.

Answer:

**Main features**

Guidance: A short list of features that exist today. Skills may describe only these.

Example: Questioning dialogue, scenario library, rubric scoring, team reports, custom scenarios

Answer:

**Integrations**

Guidance: Connections that exist today. Do not list planned ones.

Example: Single sign-on; CSV export of completion data for a learning management system

Answer:

**Data and security**

Guidance: What buyers' legal and IT teams ask: storage, training use, who sees what. Only what is true today.

Example: Conversations are kept for 90 days and are not used to train models.

Answer:

## Pricing

**Pricing model**

Guidance: How you charge. Say if the price may be published.

Example: Per learner, per year, with a minimum of 100 learners

Answer:

**Price points**

Guidance: Current prices, or "not public".

Example: $180 per learner per year

Answer:

**Trial or pilot**

Guidance: What a prospect can try before buying.

Example: An 8-week paid pilot for up to 40 learners

Answer:

## Limits

**What it is not**

Guidance: Things buyers may assume it does and it does not. Skills must not imply these.

Example: Not a general AI assistant, not a course library, not a test for hiring

Answer:

**Known weak spots**

Guidance: Honest gaps that sales hears about. Skills use these to avoid overclaiming.

Example: English only, and no voice mode

Answer:

**Roadmap you may mention**

Guidance: Only what has been cleared for public mention, with the date.

Example: Nothing cleared as of October 2026
