*Fictional example. CogniATion, CogniATion Coach, its customers, its people and its competitors are invented for this example. Numbers and quotes are made up.*

**Purpose:** The people who use, recommend, approve and block your product.

Do not fill with guesses – leave blank and the skills will ask.

## Persona 1: the champion

**Role and title**

Head of learning and development, reports to the VP of people

**What they are measured on**

Participation in programs, manager feedback, proof that training changed behavior

**Daily problem**

"Everyone finishes the course, and nobody can show me it changed how they work."

**Objections**

- "People won't make time for another training tool."
- "How would I show the leadership team that it worked?"

**Where they get information**

Peers in other companies, learning and development associations, conferences

## Persona 2: the approver

**Role and title**

VP of people

**What they are measured on**

Retention, promotion readiness, return on the training budget

**Daily problem**

"The partners keep telling me juniors can't think without the chatbot."

**Objections**

- "Is this another AI tool I have to defend to legal?"
- "Will people feel they are being watched?"

**Where they get information**

Other people leaders, industry reports, the CEO's concerns

## Persona 3: the business leader

**Role and title**

Managing partner or COO

**What they are measured on**

Quality of client work, errors, margin

**Daily problem**

"I read work that sounds confident and hasn't been thought through."

**Objections**

"Why not just tell people to check the AI's work?"

**Where they get information**

Peer networks, board members, client conversations

## Persona 4: the daily user

**Role and title**

Analyst, associate or new manager

**What they are measured on**

Quality of their work, how much rework it needs

**Daily problem**

"I get the draft back with 'why?' written on it, and I don't know how to answer."

**Objections**

"Why would I use a bot that won't give me the answer?"

**Where they get information**

Coworkers and their manager. They do not research training software.
