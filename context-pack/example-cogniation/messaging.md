## Positioning

**Positioning statement**

For learning leaders at knowledge-work companies whose staff now lean on general AI assistants, CogniATion Coach is critical thinking training software that makes people practice reasoning on real work problems, because the chatbot asks questions and declines to give the answer. Unlike Elenchly, it is practice, not a course. Unlike Arguvia, it reports progress to the learning leader and uses the customer's own scenarios.

**One-sentence description**

CogniATion Coach is an AI chatbot that won't give you the answer, so you learn to find it.

## Pillars

**Pillar 1**

It asks, it does not answer

**Proof for pillar 1**

The Coach declines to write the answer (product.md). Lena Marsh's quote from Brightmere Advisory.

**Pillar 2**

People keep practicing

**Proof for pillar 2**

72% of assigned learners completed at least one session each week in Q3 2026. Median of 9 days from signed contract to first live session.

**Pillar 3**

You can see reasoning improve

**Proof for pillar 3**

Brightmere Advisory's analysts raised their average score on client memo reasoning by 22% in one quarter of 2026, as measured by Brightmere's partners.

## Language

**Messages by persona**

- Head of learning: show that training changed how people reason, not only that they finished it.
- VP of people: build judgment in junior staff, with no individual's conversation ever on display.
- Managing partner or COO: fewer confident answers that were never thought through.
- Analyst or new manager: get better at answering "why?" before your manager asks.

**Words to use**

Reasoning, judgment, assumptions, practice, scenario

**Words to avoid**

Revolutionary, world-class, brain training, unlock your potential, smarter, superintelligent, IQ.
