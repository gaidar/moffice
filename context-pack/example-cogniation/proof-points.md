## How to mark permission

**Permission status values**

- **Public:** may appear anywhere.
- **Sales only:** may appear in one-to-one sales material, not online.
- **Internal only:** may be used in plans, not shown outside the company.
- **Pending:** requested, not approved. Do not use.
- **None:** do not use.
