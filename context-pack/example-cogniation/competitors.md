## Competitor 1

**Name and category**

Arguvia: low-cost debate practice chatbot for individuals

**Where they win**

Small teams and individuals who want something running today at a low price

**Where they lose**

Companies that need reports and their own scenarios

**Known facts, with source and date**

- Lists $9 per user per month on its pricing page (checked October 2026)
- Generic debate topics only, per its help center (checked October 2026)
- No team reports found in its help center (checked October 2026)

**Our answer**

Fine for one person practicing. Ask how they would show leadership that it worked.

## Competitor 2

**Name and category**

Elenchly: established course library on critical thinking and decision making

**Where they win**

Large companies that want one library, delivered through their learning management system

**Where they lose**

Buyers who want to see behavior change, not course completions

**Known facts, with source and date**

- Three prospects described its reports as completion data and quiz scores (won-deal and lost-deal calls, 2026)
- Pricing not public (checked October 2026)
- More than 200 video courses listed on its product pages (checked October 2026)

**Our answer**

Strong if you want a library. Ask how the last course changed the work.

## Status quo

**What buyers do today**

An annual workshop, plus telling staff to check what the AI assistant says

**Why they stay**

It seems free, and earlier training tools went unused

**Our answer**

Ask what happened after the last workshop, and how they would know.
