## What it is

**Product name**

CogniATion Coach (the Coach on second mention)

**Category**

Critical thinking training software

**What it does**

The Coach is a chatbot that questions employees about a work problem instead of answering it, then scores their reasoning against a rubric. Learners practice in sessions of about 15 minutes. Learning leaders see progress by team, and never see an individual's conversation.

**Main features**

- Questioning dialogue that asks for assumptions, evidence and alternatives, and declines to give the answer
- Library of 180 work scenarios: decisions, client memos, plans, disagreements
- Scoring on six thinking skills, using a five-point rubric
- Team-level progress reports for learning leaders
- Custom scenarios built from documents the customer uploads
- Single sign-on

**Integrations**

Single sign-on and CSV export of completion data for a learning management system. No other integrations today.

**Data and security**

Conversations are encrypted and kept for 90 days. Neither conversations nor customer documents are used to train models. Team reports go to the customer's learning leaders and VP of people. Security questionnaire available on request.

## Pricing

**Pricing model**

Per learner, per year, with a minimum of 100 learners. Prices may be published.

**Price points**

$180 per learner per year. One-time setup fee of $5,000, which includes up to 10 custom scenarios. Discount of 10% at 1,000 learners or more.

**Trial or pilot**

An 8-week paid pilot for up to 40 learners

## Limits

**What it is not**

- Not a general AI assistant. It will not write the memo or give the answer
- Not a course library or a learning management system
- Not a psychometric test, and not for hiring or performance decisions
- Makes no claim about raising intelligence or treating any condition

**Known weak spots**

English only, no voice mode, and no reports below team level, which some managers ask for.
