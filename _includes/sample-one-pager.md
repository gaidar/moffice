**Company:** CogniATion makes a specialized AI chatbot that trains employees to think through problems instead of asking for answers.

**Headline:** Your junior staff build judgment, with no conversation on display

**The problem:** Your partners keep raising the same worry: junior staff accept what the AI assistant says without checking it. That work reaches clients.

**What it does:** CogniATion Coach questions employees about a work problem instead of answering it, then scores their reasoning against a rubric. Learning leaders see progress by team, and never see an individual's conversation.

**Benefits:**

- It asks, it does not answer. The Coach asks for assumptions, evidence and alternatives, and declines to give the answer.
- People keep practicing. 72% of assigned learners completed at least one session each week in Q3 2026.
- You can see reasoning improve. Brightmere Advisory's analysts raised their average score on client memo reasoning by 22% in one quarter of 2026, as measured by Brightmere's partners.

**Customer quote:** "My analysts stopped asking the bot for the answer and started defending their own." Lena Marsh, head of learning, Brightmere Advisory

**Common questions:**

- "Is this another AI tool I have to defend to legal?": Conversations are encrypted, kept for 90 days and not used to train models. A security questionnaire is available on request.
- "Will people feel they are being watched?": Learning leaders see progress by team, never an individual's conversation. The Coach is not for performance decisions.
