Use this if you are the first person doing marketing and a founder still sells, with few or no customers. If the company has a sales team, use [First week](first-week.md) instead.

The order matters more than the speed: learn who buys and why, build the little you need to sell, test one way of reaching people, and only then plan in detail. You will finish with facts you can defend, one way of reaching buyers that you have tested, and a plan based on what happened. Time estimates are rough, and the numbers here are starting rules, not benchmarks.

## If the CEO wants a plan now

Most do, in week 1. Give them one, and say what it rests on:

1. Run [Propose quarterly goals](../skills/budget-and-planning/propose-quarterly-goals.md), which offers a learning goal when there are no numbers. The CEO chooses.
2. Run [Write a quarterly plan](../skills/budget-and-planning/write-quarterly-plan.md), giving it the day 30, 60 and 90 checks on this page as hard dates.
3. Say: "This is mostly a plan to find out. I will replan in week 12, with what we have learned."

## How to read the office at your stage

The skills and playbooks assume a company with departments. Read them like this:

| The office says | At your company |
|---|---|
| The CEO | The founder who sets direction |
| The sales lead, reps | The founder who sells, or you |
| Customers | Pilot users, prospects and people who said no |
| The CRM | A spreadsheet, one row per conversation: date, company, person, source, stage, next step, outcome |
| An MQL | Someone in your target group who asked to talk |
| An SQL | A conversation the person who sells agrees is a real opportunity |
| Pipeline | The value of those opportunities. Write down how you estimate it |

Tell a skill the same thing: "We have no CRM. This spreadsheet is our record." Some skills mark thin evidence as Hypothesis. Keep that word on it until customers remove it.

## Days 1 to 30: learn who buys and why

Two to three days a week of this, around whatever else the company needs from you.

| Week | Do this | Using | You end with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interview the founders and fill the pack | [No documents yet](../guides/fill-the-context-pack.md#no-documents-yet) | `my-context-pack.md`, with beliefs marked as beliefs |
| 1 | Set up your AI tool | [Your setup guide](../setup.md) | A project you can ask in plain words |
| 1 | Prepare and book about 10 conversations with people in your target group, including people who said no | [Write an interview guide](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/write-interview-guide.md) | A guide and booked times |
| 2 to 3 | Hold them, and start the spreadsheet | You | Notes in the buyers' words |
| 3 | Make sense of the notes | [Synthesize customer research](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/synthesize-customer-research.md) | Findings rated by how many people said each |
| 3 | Decide who you are for | [Define the ICP](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/define-icp.md), then [Write a persona](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/write-persona.md) for the one who signs | A first ICP and one persona, marked Hypothesis |
| 4 | Decide what you say | [Write a positioning statement](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/write-positioning-statement.md), then [Build a messaging framework](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/build-messaging-framework.md) | A first position, tested on the founders and two buyers |
| 4 | Decide how you sound | [Codify the brand voice](../skills/brand-and-design-briefs/codify-brand-voice.md), with no samples if you have none | A short brand voice, marked low evidence |

**Update the pack as you decide.** Paste each result into `my-context-pack.md` and upload it again. Later skills read the pack, not this chat, and would otherwise run on the founders' first beliefs.

**Day 30 check.** You can say who you are for, who you turn away, and what they do today instead, with quotes from real people. If the conversations disagree with the founders, say so now. It is the most useful thing you will find this month.

## Days 31 to 60: build the minimum and test one way in

| Week | Do this | Using | You end with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Choose the fewest tools you can work with | [Audit the tool stack](../skills/marketing-ops/audit-tool-stack.md), with no tools yet | Jobs that need a tool, each with must-haves and a price ceiling. Paste options you find, and ask for a comparison. Picking is yours |
| 5 | Agree how a lead reaches the person who sells | [Define the lead handoff](../skills/marketing-ops/define-lead-handoff.md), and [Set tracking conventions](../skills/marketing-ops/set-tracking-conventions.md) | One page of rules, and campaign names |
| 5 | Agree what these 90 days are for, if you have not yet | [Propose quarterly goals](../skills/budget-and-planning/propose-quarterly-goals.md), which offers a learning goal when there are no numbers | A goal the CEO chose |
| 6 | Make what you sell with | [Write a web page](../skills/content/write-web-page.md), [Write a sales one-pager](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-sales-one-pager.md) | A real home page and one page for sales |
| 6 | Choose one way to reach buyers | The founders' network, direct email or one community they read. Pick where the conversations came from | One channel, and what you will stop at |
| 6 to 8 | Run it as the [minimum campaign](campaign-lifecycle.md#minimum-campaign) | [Write a campaign brief](../skills/campaign-design/write-campaign-brief.md), [Write a marketing email](../skills/content/write-marketing-email.md), [Write a lead follow-up](../skills/campaign-design/write-lead-follow-up.md) | Conversations you can count |
| Weekly from 5 | Keep yourself honest | [The starter routine](weekly.md#the-starter-routine), and [Review any output](../skills/marketing-ops/review-any-output.md) before anything goes out | Three priorities a week |

**No list yet?** Build 30 to 50 names by hand from your ICP tests, in the spreadsheet, and check the rules that apply to that kind of email. The number is a starting rule. Tell the skill: "One-to-one direct email to these named people, not a list send."

**Day 60 check.** You can show how many people you reached, how many replied, how many talked and how many became real. Small numbers are fine, as long as you say they are small. A reply that says why not is worth more than a silent list.

## Days 61 to 90: learn, then plan

| Week | Do this | Using | You end with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Read what the test showed | [Evaluate a campaign](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/evaluate-campaign.md) | Keep, change or stop, with the reason |
| 9 | Update the facts | [Review messaging](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/review-messaging.md), then edit `my-context-pack.md` | Hypotheses confirmed or dropped |
| 10 | Get your first proof | [Request customer proof](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/request-customer-proof.md) | Permission asked, in writing, from everyone who liked it |
| 10 to 11 | Run the test again, changing one thing | Same skills as weeks 6 to 8 | A second result to compare |
| 12 | Plan the next quarter | [Write a quarterly plan](../skills/budget-and-planning/write-quarterly-plan.md), [Allocate the budget](../skills/budget-and-planning/allocate-budget.md) | A plan based on what you saw |
| 12 | Say what you would hire first | The tasks you still do by hand, and those you do badly | A one-line recommendation for the CEO |

**Day 90 check.** The CEO has a plan, a budget and a goal, each tied to something you observed. You know one way of reaching buyers that works a little, or you know that it does not and what you will try next.

## What to skip, and until when

There are 57 skills, and these 90 days use 23 of them. Leave the others alone, including the ones below, until the reason stops being true. You are not behind.

| Skip | Until |
|---|---|
| [Build a market segmentation](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/build-market-segmentation.md) | You have about 20 customers to compare |
| [Analyze wins and losses](../skills/competitive-intelligence/analyze-win-loss.md) | Enough closed deals to see a pattern. Until then, ask each person why |
| [Write a battlecard](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-battlecard.md), [Write a competitor profile](../skills/competitive-intelligence/write-competitor-profile.md) | The same rival comes up in about three deals. What buyers do today is your real competitor |
| [Plan a paid campaign](../skills/paid-demand-gen/plan-paid-campaign.md) and the other paid skills | You have a page and a message that have worked without paying for attention |
| [Audit SEO from exports](../skills/seo/audit-seo-from-exports.md) and the other SEO skills | The site has traffic to read. Search takes months, so it is not your first test |
| [Read attribution](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/read-attribution.md), [Specify a dashboard](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/specify-dashboard.md) | The spreadsheet stops being enough |
| [Design a nurture sequence](../skills/campaign-design/design-nurture-sequence.md) | You have leads who are not ready, in numbers you cannot follow by hand |
| [Write a press release](../skills/pr-and-comms/write-press-release.md), [Write a media pitch](../skills/pr-and-comms/write-media-pitch.md) | You have real news and a customer who will say so. Keep [Draft a holding statement](../skills/pr-and-comms/draft-holding-statement.md) in mind |
| [Plan an event](../skills/events-and-webinars/plan-event.md), [Plan a webinar](../skills/events-and-webinars/plan-webinar.md) | You have something proven to say and a list to invite |
| [Build a partner kit](../skills/partner-and-channel-marketing/build-partner-kit.md), [Plan co-marketing with a partner](../skills/partner-and-channel-marketing/plan-co-marketing.md) | The sale works without a partner, or a partner asks |
| [Write a blog post](../skills/content/write-blog-post.md), [Write social posts](../skills/content/write-social-posts.md), [Write a video script](../skills/content/write-video-script.md) | You have a message buyers respond to. Before that, content spreads a guess |

You do not need the 14 agent roles either. Ask in plain words, and use the head of marketing when you are unsure what comes next.

## What to tell the CEO

Say it or send it at days 30, 60 and 90:

```
I talked to <number> people in <target group>. Most said <finding, and how many said it>.
Our best guess at who buys is <ICP>, because <reason>. This is a hypothesis until <what would confirm it>.
We tried <one way of reaching buyers>: <reached>, <replied>, <talked>, <real opportunities>.
Next I will <one thing>, and I will know more by <date>.
I need from you: <a decision, an introduction or a number>.
```

Do not forecast pipeline at day 30 or day 60. You have nothing to base it on, and the number will be repeated as a promise.

## Worked example: CogniATion in its first year

This is CogniATion before the [example pack](../context-pack/example-cogniation/company.md) existed. Everything here is illustrative.

**Founder interview.** The founders believe buyers are chief learning officers at large banks, because that is who they know. The pack records it as "founder belief".

**Ten conversations.** Seven say the same thing: everyone finishes the course, and nobody can show it changed how people work. Those seven run learning at firms of 300 to 5,000 people, mostly professional services. The large banks want a security review before a first call. The synthesis rates the first finding Strong, and the ICP hypothesis moves to professional services firms.

**One way in.** The founders know few people there, so the marketer emails 40 heads of learning directly, under the brief from the minimum campaign, after checking the rules that apply to that kind of email. 40 reached, 6 replied, 4 talked, 1 real opportunity. Two of the replies said "not this year", and why.

**Day 60 to the CEO.**

```
I talked to 10 people in learning teams. 7 said they cannot show that training changed how people work.
Our best guess at who buys is heads of learning at professional services firms of 300 to 5,000 people, because that is where the 7 sit. This is a hypothesis until we have three paying customers there.
We tried direct email: 40 reached, 6 replied, 4 talked, 1 real opportunity.
Next I will run it again with a new first line, and I will know more by June 12.
I need from you: an introduction to the two partners you know at Brightmere Advisory.
```

Note what it leaves out: no market size, no forecast and no claim that email works. One real opportunity from 40 is a small number, and it says so by showing it.
