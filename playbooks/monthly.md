Run this in the first week of each month, in place of some of that week's content work. Every output is a draft, and you, the marketer, decide what to do with it. Time estimates are rough.

## Steps

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Input you bring | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Write the monthly report](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/write-monthly-report.md) | Marketing analyst | Month's numbers from your tools | 45 minutes |
| 2 | [Evaluate a campaign](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/evaluate-campaign.md), for each campaign that ended | Marketing analyst | Campaign brief and results | 30 minutes each |
| 3 | [Read attribution](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/read-attribution.md) | Marketing analyst | First-touch and last-touch export | 30 minutes |
| 4 | [Audit SEO from exports](../skills/seo/audit-seo-from-exports.md) | SEO specialist | Search and crawl exports | 45 minutes |
| 5 | [Write a competitor profile](../skills/competitive-intelligence/write-competitor-profile.md), updating your top competitors | Product marketing manager | Anything you or sales have seen, pasted in | 30 minutes |
| 6 | [Plan a content calendar](../skills/content/plan-content-calendar.md) for the next month | Content lead | Monthly report, quarterly plan | 30 minutes |
| 7 | Check the context pack, following [Maintain and update](../guides/maintain-and-update.md) | You | Your context pack | 30 minutes |
| 8 | [Set weekly priorities](../skills/budget-and-planning/set-weekly-priorities.md), using the monthly report | Head of marketing | Report from step 1 | 15 minutes |

## Share

Send the monthly report to the people who need it. Read it first: the commentary is a draft, and the decisions in it are yours to make.

## Exit check

- You can state pipeline against target in one sentence.
- Each finished campaign has a verdict, and next actions marked keep, change or stop.
- Next month's calendar exists.
- The context pack has been checked.

## If time is short

Do steps 1, 6 and 7.
