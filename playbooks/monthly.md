Run this in the first week of each month, in place of some of that week's content work. It takes about 2 to 3 hours, plus 30 minutes for each finished campaign. Time estimates are rough.

Attribution and the SEO audit run in [the quarterly playbook](quarterly.md), because a month is shorter than most B2B sales cycles. Competitor updates run when something changes, as below.

## Steps

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Input you bring | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Write the monthly report](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/write-monthly-report.md) | Marketing analyst | Month's numbers from your tools | 45 minutes |
| 2 | [Diagnose a pipeline gap](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/diagnose-pipeline-gap.md), only where the report shows pipeline behind target | Marketing analyst | Report from step 1, CRM numbers | 30 minutes |
| 3 | [Evaluate a campaign](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/evaluate-campaign.md), for each campaign that ended | Marketing analyst | Campaign brief and results | 30 minutes each |
| 4 | [Plan a content calendar](../skills/content/plan-content-calendar.md) for the next month | Content lead | Monthly report, quarterly plan | 30 minutes |
| 5 | Check the context pack, following [Maintain and update](../guides/maintain-and-update.md) | You | Your context pack | 30 minutes |
| 6 | [Set weekly priorities](../skills/budget-and-planning/set-weekly-priorities.md), using the monthly report | Head of marketing | Report from step 1 | 15 minutes |

One person runs one launch or one major campaign at a time. If the month's report or calendar puts two in the same weeks, move one.

## When something changes

Do not update competitors on a schedule. Run [Write a competitor profile](../skills/competitive-intelligence/write-competitor-profile.md) (product marketing manager, about 30 minutes) when a rival changes its price or product, starts appearing in deals, or sales reports a new claim. Paste in what you or sales have seen, with the date.

## Share

Send the monthly report to the people who need it. Read it first: the commentary is a draft, and the decisions in it are yours to make.

## Exit check

- You can state pipeline against target in one sentence, and, if it is behind, say whether volume, conversion or deal size is the cause.
- Each finished campaign has a verdict, and next actions marked keep, change or stop.
- Next month's calendar exists.
- The context pack has been checked.

## If time is short

Do steps 1, 4 and 5.
