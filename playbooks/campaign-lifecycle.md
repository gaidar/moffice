Run this for each campaign. Every output is a draft, and you, the marketer, decide what runs and when to stop it.

## Sequence

| Stage | Step | Skill or action | Owner role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brief | 1 | [Write a campaign brief](../skills/campaign-design/write-campaign-brief.md), with one goal, one audience and one time window | Demand generation manager |
| Brief | 2 | [Review any output](../skills/marketing-ops/review-any-output.md) on the brief | Marketing reviewer |
| Plan | 3 | [Plan a paid campaign](../skills/paid-demand-gen/plan-paid-campaign.md), if the brief uses paid | Paid media manager |
| Plan | 4 | [Plan a webinar](../skills/events-and-webinars/plan-webinar.md) or [Plan an event](../skills/events-and-webinars/plan-event.md), if the brief uses one | Events and webinar manager |
| Plan | 5 | [Plan co-marketing with a partner](../skills/partner-and-channel-marketing/plan-co-marketing.md), if a partner is involved | Partner marketing manager |
| Plan | 6 | [Set tracking conventions](../skills/marketing-ops/set-tracking-conventions.md): name the campaign and build its links | Marketing operations manager |
| Make | 7 | [Write a design brief](../skills/brand-and-design-briefs/write-design-brief.md) for anything visual | Brand and creative director |
| Make | 8 | [Write a web page](../skills/content/write-web-page.md) for the landing page | Content lead |
| Make | 9 | [Write ad variations](../skills/paid-demand-gen/write-ad-variations.md), [Write a marketing email](../skills/content/write-marketing-email.md), [Write social posts](../skills/content/write-social-posts.md) as the brief calls for | Paid media manager, content lead |
| Make | 10 | [Design a nurture sequence](../skills/campaign-design/design-nurture-sequence.md) for leads not ready for sales | Demand generation manager |
| Check | 11 | [Review any output](../skills/marketing-ops/review-any-output.md) on every public output. Send to approvers in channels-and-tools.md | Marketing reviewer, you |
| Run | 12 | Launch in your own tools. The office cannot do this for you. Confirm the lead handoff with sales | You |
| Run | 13 | [Adjust paid campaigns](../skills/paid-demand-gen/adjust-paid-campaigns.md) weekly, if paid is running | Paid media manager |
| Close | 14 | [Follow up after an event](../skills/events-and-webinars/follow-up-after-event.md), if there was one | Events and webinar manager |
| Close | 15 | [Evaluate a campaign](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/evaluate-campaign.md) when the window ends and leads have had time to mature | Marketing analyst |
| Close | 16 | Add results to proof-points.md if they are usable and permitted. Use the evaluation in the next brief | You |

## Exit check

- The brief stated a target, and the evaluation reports against it.
- Every public output passed review and its approver.
- The evaluation ends in a verdict and keep, change or stop actions that you have accepted or overruled.
