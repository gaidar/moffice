**First time? Start with [Propose quarterly goals](../skills/budget-and-planning/propose-quarterly-goals.md), which is step 9, then run steps 10, 11, 14 and 15.** Add step 1 if you have a CRM export of closed deals, and step 12 for each campaign in the plan. The rest of week 1 reads a history you may not have yet, so leave it for next quarter, along with the first two lines of the exit check. If you are starting mid-quarter, run this now rather than waiting for the last two weeks.

Otherwise, run this in the last two weeks of the quarter. The people who approve the plan and budget are named in channels-and-tools.md. Time estimates are rough.

## Week 1: look back

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Input you bring | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Analyze wins and losses](../skills/competitive-intelligence/analyze-win-loss.md) | Product marketing manager | CRM export, call notes | 1 hour |
| 2 | [Synthesize customer research](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/synthesize-customer-research.md) | Insights analyst | Notes from your customer conversations this quarter, and any survey or call notes | 1 hour |
| 3 | [Read attribution](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/read-attribution.md) | Marketing analyst | First-touch and last-touch export | 30 minutes |
| 4 | [Audit SEO from exports](../skills/seo/audit-seo-from-exports.md) | SEO specialist | Search and crawl exports | 45 minutes |
| 5 | [Diagnose a pipeline gap](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/diagnose-pipeline-gap.md), only if the quarter's pipeline is behind target | Marketing analyst | Quarter's CRM numbers | 30 minutes |
| 6 | [Define the ICP](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/define-icp.md), updating it from steps 1 and 2 | Insights analyst | Customer list with outcomes | 45 minutes |
| 7 | [Review messaging](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/review-messaging.md) against what you learned | Marketing reviewer | Findings from steps 1 and 2 | 30 minutes |
| 8 | [Audit the tool stack](../skills/marketing-ops/audit-tool-stack.md) | Marketing operations manager | Tool list and costs | 30 minutes |

## Week 2: plan forward

One person runs one launch or one major campaign at a time. Plan the quarter so they follow each other, not overlap.

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Input you bring | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | [Propose quarterly goals](../skills/budget-and-planning/propose-quarterly-goals.md), then take the options to the CEO, who decides. Update goals-and-kpis.md with their choice. If the CEO has already set the goals, just update the file | Head of marketing drafts, you and the CEO decide | Last quarter's numbers, the company's targets | 30 minutes, then 1 hour with the CEO |
| 10 | [Write a quarterly plan](../skills/budget-and-planning/write-quarterly-plan.md) | Head of marketing | Goals, last quarter's numbers | 1 hour |
| 11 | [Allocate the budget](../skills/budget-and-planning/allocate-budget.md) | Head of marketing | Budget, channel results, attribution from step 3 | 30 minutes |
| 12 | [Write a campaign brief](../skills/campaign-design/write-campaign-brief.md), for each campaign in the plan | Demand generation manager | The plan | 30 minutes each |
| 13 | [Plan a product launch](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/plan-product-launch.md), for any release next quarter | Product marketing manager | Release facts from product | 45 minutes each |
| 14 | [Review any output](../skills/marketing-ops/review-any-output.md) on the plan, then read it yourself with [Judge an output](../guides/judge-an-output.md) | Marketing reviewer, then you | The plan | 45 minutes |
| 15 | Present the plan and get budget approval | You | The plan | Your call |

**Before step 15.** The review in step 14 checks facts, permissions and format. It does not tell you whether the plan is wise. That is the job of [Judge an output](../guides/judge-an-output.md), which gives you the questions a senior person would ask, such as "What would have to be true?" and "What will you do if it fails?"

## Exit check

- messaging.md and icp-and-segments.md match what you learned.
- Attribution and the SEO audit have been read, and their findings are in the plan.
- goals-and-kpis.md shows the new quarter.
- The plan has been approved, with no launch and major campaign overlapping, and each campaign has a brief.
- The first month's calendar, from [Plan a content calendar](../skills/content/plan-content-calendar.md), is ready.

## If time is short

Do steps 1, 9, 10 and 11.
