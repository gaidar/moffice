Run this in the last two weeks of the quarter. Every output is a draft, and you, the marketer, decide. The people who approve the plan and budget are named in channels-and-tools.md. Time estimates are rough.

## Week 1: look back

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Input you bring | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Analyze wins and losses](../skills/competitive-intelligence/analyze-win-loss.md) | Product marketing manager | CRM export, call notes | 1 hour |
| 2 | [Synthesize customer research](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/synthesize-customer-research.md) | Insights analyst | Interview, survey or call notes | 1 hour |
| 3 | [Define the ICP](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/define-icp.md), updating it from steps 1 and 2 | Insights analyst | Customer list with outcomes | 45 minutes |
| 4 | [Review messaging](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/review-messaging.md) against what you learned | Marketing reviewer | Findings from steps 1 and 2 | 30 minutes |
| 5 | [Audit the tool stack](../skills/marketing-ops/audit-tool-stack.md) | Marketing operations manager | Tool list and costs | 30 minutes |

## Week 2: plan forward

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Input you bring | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Update goals-and-kpis.md with next quarter's company goals | You, with the CEO | The company's targets | 1 hour |
| 7 | [Write a quarterly plan](../skills/budget-and-planning/write-quarterly-plan.md) | Head of marketing | Goals, last quarter's numbers | 1 hour |
| 8 | [Allocate the budget](../skills/budget-and-planning/allocate-budget.md) | Head of marketing | Budget, channel results | 30 minutes |
| 9 | [Write a campaign brief](../skills/campaign-design/write-campaign-brief.md), for each campaign in the plan | Demand generation manager | The plan | 30 minutes each |
| 10 | [Plan a product launch](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/plan-product-launch.md), for any release next quarter | Product marketing manager | Release facts from product | 45 minutes each |
| 11 | [Review any output](../skills/marketing-ops/review-any-output.md) on the plan | Marketing reviewer | The plan | 15 minutes |
| 12 | Present the plan and get budget approval | You | The plan | Your call |

## Exit check

- messaging.md and icp-and-segments.md match what you learned.
- goals-and-kpis.md shows the new quarter.
- The plan has been approved, and each campaign has a brief.
- The first month's calendar, from [Plan a content calendar](../skills/content/plan-content-calendar.md), is ready.

## If time is short

Do steps 1, 6, 7 and 8.
