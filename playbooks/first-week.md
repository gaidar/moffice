Run this once, after [Start here](../guides/start-here.md). Each output is a draft, and you, the marketer, decide what to keep. The days are a suggestion. Skip what you already have.

## Before you begin

The context pack is filled in as far as you can take it. Blanks are acceptable.

## Day by day

| Day | Goal | Skill or action | Owner role | Output you keep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check foundations | [Write a positioning statement](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/write-positioning-statement.md), only if messaging.md is blank or weak | Product marketing manager | Updated messaging.md |
| 1 | Check foundations | [Review messaging](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/review-messaging.md) on what is in messaging.md | Marketing reviewer | A list of fixes |
| 2 | Know the buyer | [Define the ICP](../skills/segmentation-icp-and-personas/define-icp.md), if icp-and-segments.md was hard to fill in | Insights analyst | Updated icp-and-segments.md |
| 2 | Know the competition | [Write a competitor profile](../skills/competitive-intelligence/write-competitor-profile.md) for your top competitor | Product marketing manager | Updated competitors.md |
| 3 | Set the quarter | [Write a quarterly plan](../skills/budget-and-planning/write-quarterly-plan.md) | Head of marketing | Quarterly plan |
| 3 | Set the money | [Allocate the budget](../skills/budget-and-planning/allocate-budget.md) | Head of marketing | Budget table |
| 4 | Get tracking right | [Set tracking conventions](../skills/marketing-ops/set-tracking-conventions.md) | Marketing operations manager | Tracking sheet |
| 4 | Fix the handoff | [Define the lead handoff](../skills/marketing-ops/define-lead-handoff.md) | Marketing operations manager | Handoff rules, agreed with sales |
| 5 | Ship something | [Write a sales one-pager](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-sales-one-pager.md), then [Review any output](../skills/marketing-ops/review-any-output.md) | Product marketing manager, marketing reviewer | One output ready for your approvers |
| 5 | Plan next week | [Set weekly priorities](../skills/budget-and-planning/set-weekly-priorities.md) | Head of marketing | Next week's priorities |

## Exit check

- Messaging, ICP and top competitor are written down, and you agree with them.
- The quarter has goals, a budget and a plan.
- Tracking conventions and handoff rules exist.
- One output has gone through a full draft, review and approval.
