Pick the sequence by tier, which you set with [Plan a product launch](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/plan-product-launch.md), and decide the tier and date yourself. Tier 3 is small, about 2 hours of work. Tier 2 is medium, about 2 days over 4 weeks. Tier 1 is large, and takes about half your time for 8 weeks. These are rough estimates.

One person runs one launch or one major campaign at a time. If a launch and a major campaign fall in the same weeks, move one, or shrink one to the small launch or the minimum campaign in [the campaign lifecycle](campaign-lifecycle.md).

Two dates matter in every sequence that has them. Ask customers for proof early, since their approval takes weeks. Brief sales two weeks before launch day, before anything public.

## Small launch: tier 3

A fix or minor improvement.

| When | Step | Skill or action |
|---|---|---|
| 2 weeks before | 1 | [Plan a product launch](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/plan-product-launch.md), which confirms the tier |
| 1 week before | 2 | Send sales a short note on what changes for buyers |
| 1 week before | 3 | [Write a marketing email](../skills/content/write-marketing-email.md) for a mention in the next newsletter, and a short release note |
| Launch day | 4 | Publish and send. The office cannot do this for you |

## Medium launch: tier 2

A new capability for current customers or one persona, or a fix to a known weak spot.

| When | Step | Skill or action |
|---|---|---|
| 4 weeks before | 1 | [Plan a product launch](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/plan-product-launch.md), which sets the tier and scope |
| 4 weeks before, or as early as 8 if you know the date | 2 | [Request customer proof](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/request-customer-proof.md), if the release needs a new quote or number |
| 3 weeks before | 3 | [Write a sales one-pager](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-sales-one-pager.md), or update the current one |
| 3 weeks before | 4 | [Write a web page](../skills/content/write-web-page.md) to update the product page |
| 2 weeks before | 5 | Brief sales, using the one-pager |
| 2 weeks before | 6 | [Write a marketing email](../skills/content/write-marketing-email.md), [Write social posts](../skills/content/write-social-posts.md) |
| 1 week before | 7 | [Review launch readiness](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/review-launch-readiness.md). You make the go or no-go call |
| Launch day | 8 | Publish and send. The office cannot do this for you |
| 4 weeks after | 9 | [Evaluate a campaign](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/evaluate-campaign.md). Update product.md, messaging.md and proof-points.md |

## Large launch: tier 1

The release changes who can buy or the price, adds a new product, or makes a new claim under a messaging pillar.

| When | Step | Skill or action |
|---|---|---|
| 8 weeks before | 1 | [Plan a product launch](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/plan-product-launch.md), which sets the tier and scope |
| 8 weeks before | 2 | [Write a go-to-market plan](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/write-gtm-plan.md), only for a new product, segment or market |
| 8 weeks before | 3 | [Request customer proof](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/request-customer-proof.md) for what the launch needs to show |
| 7 weeks before | 4 | [Build a messaging framework](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/build-messaging-framework.md) for what is new, then [Review messaging](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/review-messaging.md) |
| 6 weeks before | 5 | [Write a campaign brief](../skills/campaign-design/write-campaign-brief.md) |
| 5 weeks before | 6 | [Write a design brief](../skills/brand-and-design-briefs/write-design-brief.md) |
| 4 weeks before | 7 | [Write a web page](../skills/content/write-web-page.md), [Write a blog post](../skills/content/write-blog-post.md), [Write a video script](../skills/content/write-video-script.md) |
| 4 weeks before | 8 | [Write a sales one-pager](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-sales-one-pager.md), [Write a battlecard](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-battlecard.md), and [Write a sales deck](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-sales-deck.md) if the first call changes |
| 3 weeks before | 9 | [Write a press release](../skills/pr-and-comms/write-press-release.md), [Write a media pitch](../skills/pr-and-comms/write-media-pitch.md), [Draft a holding statement](../skills/pr-and-comms/draft-holding-statement.md) for what could go wrong |
| 3 weeks before | 10 | [Plan a webinar](../skills/events-and-webinars/plan-webinar.md), [Write ad variations](../skills/paid-demand-gen/write-ad-variations.md) |
| 2 weeks before | 11 | Brief sales, using the sales deck if you wrote one, otherwise the one-pager and battlecard |
| 2 weeks before | 12 | [Write a marketing email](../skills/content/write-marketing-email.md), [Write social posts](../skills/content/write-social-posts.md), [Build a partner kit](../skills/partner-and-channel-marketing/build-partner-kit.md) if partners sell it |
| 1 week before | 13 | [Review launch readiness](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/review-launch-readiness.md). You make the go or no-go call |
| Launch day | 14 | Publish and send. The office cannot do this for you |
| 2 weeks after | 15 | [Adjust paid campaigns](../skills/paid-demand-gen/adjust-paid-campaigns.md); [Follow up after an event](../skills/events-and-webinars/follow-up-after-event.md) for webinar leads |
| 4 weeks after | 16 | [Evaluate a campaign](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/evaluate-campaign.md). Update product.md, messaging.md and proof-points.md |

## Exit check

- Product claims were approved by the person named in channels-and-tools.md.
- Sales was briefed two weeks before launch day for a medium or large launch, and before launch day for a small one.
- Any customer proof used has approved permission in proof-points.md. A pending request stays out of the launch.
- Every link has tracking, per the conventions from [Set tracking conventions](../skills/marketing-ops/set-tracking-conventions.md).
- The context pack shows the new facts.
