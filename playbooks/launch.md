Run this for each release that earns a launch. Every output is a draft, and you, the marketer, decide the launch tier and the date. The timing below assumes a large launch. Compress it for a small one. Tier comes from step 1.

## Sequence

| When | Step | Skill or action | Owner role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 weeks before | 1 | [Plan a product launch](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/plan-product-launch.md), which sets the tier and scope | Product marketing manager |
| 8 weeks before | 2 | [Write a go-to-market plan](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/write-gtm-plan.md), only for a new product, segment or market | Product marketing manager |
| 7 weeks before | 3 | [Build a messaging framework](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/build-messaging-framework.md) for what is new, then [Review messaging](../skills/positioning-and-messaging/review-messaging.md) | Product marketing manager, marketing reviewer |
| 6 weeks before | 4 | [Write a campaign brief](../skills/campaign-design/write-campaign-brief.md) | Demand generation manager |
| 5 weeks before | 5 | [Write a design brief](../skills/brand-and-design-briefs/write-design-brief.md) | Brand and creative director |
| 4 weeks before | 6 | [Write a web page](../skills/content/write-web-page.md), [Write a blog post](../skills/content/write-blog-post.md), [Write a video script](../skills/content/write-video-script.md) | Content lead |
| 4 weeks before | 7 | [Write a sales one-pager](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-sales-one-pager.md), [Write a battlecard](../skills/product-marketing-and-sales-enablement/write-battlecard.md) | Product marketing manager |
| 3 weeks before | 8 | [Write a press release](../skills/pr-and-comms/write-press-release.md), [Write a media pitch](../skills/pr-and-comms/write-media-pitch.md), [Draft a holding statement](../skills/pr-and-comms/draft-holding-statement.md) for what could go wrong | PR and communications manager |
| 3 weeks before | 9 | [Plan a webinar](../skills/events-and-webinars/plan-webinar.md), [Write ad variations](../skills/paid-demand-gen/write-ad-variations.md) | Events and webinar manager, paid media manager |
| 2 weeks before | 10 | [Write a marketing email](../skills/content/write-marketing-email.md), [Write social posts](../skills/content/write-social-posts.md), [Build a partner kit](../skills/partner-and-channel-marketing/build-partner-kit.md) if partners sell it | Content lead, partner marketing manager |
| 1 week before | 11 | [Review launch readiness](../skills/go-to-market-and-launches/review-launch-readiness.md). You make the go or no-go call, then brief sales | Marketing reviewer, you |
| Launch day | 12 | Publish and send. The office cannot do this for you | You |
| 2 weeks after | 13 | [Adjust paid campaigns](../skills/paid-demand-gen/adjust-paid-campaigns.md); [Follow up after an event](../skills/events-and-webinars/follow-up-after-event.md) for webinar leads | Paid media manager, events and webinar manager |
| 4 weeks after | 14 | [Evaluate a campaign](../skills/analytics-and-campaign-evaluation/evaluate-campaign.md). Update product.md, messaging.md and proof-points.md | Marketing analyst, you |

## Exit check

- Product claims were approved by the person named in channels-and-tools.md.
- Sales was briefed before launch day.
- Every link has tracking, per the conventions from [Set tracking conventions](../skills/marketing-ops/set-tracking-conventions.md).
- The context pack shows the new facts.
