Run this every week. Every output is a draft, and you, the marketer, decide what to do with it. Time estimates are rough and assume the context pack is filled in.

## Monday: decide

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pull last week's numbers from your tools: pipeline, leads, paid spend, top pages | You | 20 minutes |
| 2 | [Set weekly priorities](../skills/budget-and-planning/set-weekly-priorities.md), pasting in the numbers and this quarter's plan | Head of marketing | 15 minutes |
| 3 | [Adjust paid campaigns](../skills/paid-demand-gen/adjust-paid-campaigns.md), if you run paid, pasting in a performance export | Paid media manager | 20 minutes |

## Tuesday to Thursday: make

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Write this week's items from the calendar you made with [Plan a content calendar](../skills/content/plan-content-calendar.md): [Write a blog post](../skills/content/write-blog-post.md), [Write social posts](../skills/content/write-social-posts.md), [Write a marketing email](../skills/content/write-marketing-email.md) | Content lead | 2 to 4 hours |
| 5 | Run the priority work for the week, using the skill it calls for | The role that owns it | Varies |
| 6 | [Review any output](../skills/marketing-ops/review-any-output.md) for anything public or using a customer name or number | Marketing reviewer | 10 minutes per item |
| 7 | Send items to the approvers listed in channels-and-tools.md | You | 15 minutes |

## Friday: close

| Step | Skill or action | Owner role | Time (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Publish, send and hand over what was approved. The office cannot do this for you | You | 1 hour |
| 9 | Note what changed: new proof points, a competitor move, a sales objection. Update the context pack if it is a fact | You | 15 minutes |

## Exit check

- Up to three priorities were set, and you know whether each got done.
- Everything public went through its approver.
- Any new facts are in the context pack.

## If you only have two hours

Do steps 1 and 2, and only the top priority. Skip the rest and say so in next week's input.
